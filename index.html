🌍 Getting Started

Clean Energy Atlas is an interactive global map of clean energy infrastructure. Use the Layers panel on the left to toggle energy types, the transmission grid, and the project pipeline.

📋 Project Pipeline

Shows 461 proposed projects (305 GW) seeking grid interconnection approvals across the US, UK, Australia, Canada, Germany, India, China, and more. Covers CAISO, ERCOT, SPP, and ISONE queues. Data refreshes monthly.

Ring color = approval stage  ·  Dot color = energy type
